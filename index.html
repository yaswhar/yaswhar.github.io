R2C2R: Risk-Sensitive Riccati-Structured Critic with Certified Robustness
Yashar Zafari, MJ. Shamseddin, Y. Tahmasebzadeh
arXiv preprint in preparation, 2026
arXiv (coming soon)

Learns risk-sensitive control as a principal eigenpair with a Riccati-structured RL critic, then certifies two-sided bounds on the regional risk-sensitive rate that remain valid beyond the breakdown point, computed with outward rounding. Validated on the inverted pendulum and cart-pole. In the animation, a designed test function on a noisy pendulum closes the certified bracket to 0.23% of the true rate, and the final brackets are re-checked in ball arithmetic. The core lemmas are checked in the Lean proof assistant.

 Learning to Interpret and Execute Iranian Sign Language via Skill-Based Deep Reinforcement Learning: An Offline-to-Online, Cross-Lingual Framework for Robotic Deployment
M.Sc. thesis, Sharif University of Technology · in progress
Advisors: Prof. Alireza Taheri and Prof. Ali Meghdari

I'm building a system that both reads Iranian Sign Language and performs it on RASA, a 36-actuator humanoid with dexterous hands and an animated screen face. I built RASA's simulation in MuJoCo and a library that retargets human sign-language motion (3.5M frames, 6 datasets) onto it, preserving handshape, hand location relative to the face and body, and palm orientation within the robot's joint limits and without self-collision. On held-out signers, a sign classifier recognizes RASA's replays at 75% of its accuracy on human video, the ceiling for the RL executor now in development.

Selected predictions on synthetic validation renders.

 RoboPose: Multi-Robot Pose Estimation from Synthetic Data
Individual project · Fall 2026 · in progress

One real-time model that detects robots of four classes (humanoid, quadruped, arm, quadcopter; 34 models) and their landmarks, trained only on randomized MuJoCo renders. Synthetic validation: pose mAP 0.61, precision 0.99, recall 0.94; real-image evaluation in progress.

Simulation of Dexterous Robotic Hand Object Grasping Control Using Reinforcement Learning
B.Sc. thesis, Sharif University of Technology · 2025
Advisor: Prof. Alireza Taheri
code

A grasping pipeline for a 26-DoF Shadow Hand on a UR10e in MuJoCo, combining GraspAnything grasp-point detection, trajectory planning, and PPO finger control: 88% success on training shapes, 69% on 8 unseen objects. Code and models are open source.

Next step: a ROS2 driver for RASA's new hand (Spring 2026), the basis for transferring this grasping policy from simulation to hardware. Paused while the hardware is under repair.

 LiDAR-Guided Parallel Mechanism with Custom Cycloidal Gearboxes
Freelance design project · Fall 2026

Designed and built a four-actuator, 2-DoF parallel mechanism that positions and rotates a plate so it sits tangent to the ground below it. A 2D LiDAR scans the ground geometry to set the plate's target height and tilt, and inverse kinematics gives the matching motor positions. Each actuator drives through a custom single-stage 11:1 cycloidal gearbox with a 3D-printed disc and housing, steel pins and bearings.
